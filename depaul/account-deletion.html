Account Deletion Request

Use this form to request deletion of your DePaul Em School Yerravaram app account and associated personal data. Developed by Sesh Rao, we will process your request within 7 business days.

⚠ Important: Deleting your account only removes your login access to the DePaul Em School Yerravaram app. Your academic records (marks, attendance, fees) are maintained by the school and are not deleted — please contact the school directly for academic record removal.

What gets deleted

  • Your mobile app login credentials and OTP session
  • All active login sessions and tokens
  • Your parent account profile stored in our system
  • Parent–child links associated with your account
  • Your device binding record

What is retained

  • Academic records (marks, attendance) — maintained by DePaul Em School Yerravaram
  • Fee payment receipts — retained for financial compliance (7 years)
