Account Deletion Request

Use this form to request deletion of your Ascent Schools mobile app account and associated personal data. We will process your request within 7 business days.

⚠️ Important: Deleting your mobile app account only removes your login access to the Ascent Schools app. Your academic records (marks, attendance, fees) are maintained by your school and are not deleted — please contact your school directly for academic record removal.

What gets deleted

  • Your mobile app login credentials (PIN)
  • All active login sessions and tokens
  • Your account profile stored in our system
  • Parent–child links (for parent accounts)

What is retained

  • Academic records (marks, attendance) — maintained by your school
  • Fee payment receipts — retained for financial compliance (7 years)
