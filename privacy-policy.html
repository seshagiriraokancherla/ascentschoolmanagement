Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Ascent Info Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Ascent Schools mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Account credentials — admission number, mobile number, and PIN (stored as a secure hash)
  • Student information — name, class, date of birth, gender, blood group, address (read from your school's records)
  • Contact information — parent/guardian mobile number and email address
  • Financial information — fee payment history and receipt details
  • Attendance records — daily attendance status
  • Academic records — exam marks and homework details
  • Device information — device type and operating system version (for app compatibility)

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and provide secure access to the App
  • Display your child's academic, attendance, and fee information
  • Process fee payments through the integrated payment gateway (Razorpay)
  • Send notifications regarding fees, homework, and school announcements
  • Improve the performance and reliability of the App

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • School administration — student and payment data is shared with the respective school to maintain records
  • Payment processor — when you make a fee payment, transaction details are shared with Razorpay to process the payment securely
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or a valid government order

4. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
  • Passwords and PINs are stored as SHA-256 hashes — never in plain text
  • Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted storage on your device (Android EncryptedSharedPreferences)
  • Access tokens expire automatically and are rotated on each session

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your school's account is active on the Ascent Schools platform. When a school's subscription ends, data is retained for 90 days before being permanently deleted, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is used to view information about students, including minors. Student data is provided by and managed by the school administration. Parents and guardians access their child's information through verified parent accounts linked by the school. We do not knowingly allow minors to create independent accounts without school-verified parental consent.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data (contact your school administration)
  • Deletion — request deletion of your mobile account by contacting us at the email below
  • Opt-out — uninstall the App at any time; this does not affect your school records

8. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Razorpay — payment processing

These services have their own privacy policies and data practices that are independent of ours.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
